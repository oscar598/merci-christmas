Questions

Good to Know

Does the person I send it to need the app?

No. If they have merci., the card opens right inside Messages. If not, or if they're on a Mac, the bubble opens the card in their browser.

Can I use my own photos and handwriting?

Yes. Upload photos or a video, then sign with your finger or snap a photo of your signature and we'll clean it up.

Is my card public?

No, not unless you choose to share the design. Shared copies have your photos and signature removed.

What holidays are coming?

Thanksgiving is live now. Christmas, Hanukkah and New Year are next, along with thank-you notes, invitations and wedding cards.
