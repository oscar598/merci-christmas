# merci. Thanksgiving: animated thank-you and Thanksgiving cards for iMessage

merci. lets you design an animated Thanksgiving card on your iPhone or the web, sign it by hand, put a gift inside, and send it in Messages. The recipient taps the bubble and the card opens in the chat.

## Where it works

iMessage on iPhone and iPad, Mac (opens in the browser), any web browser, and rich link previews when sent as a link.

## How it works

1. **Pick a design.** Ten Thanksgiving designs, from very still to very lively.
2. **Make it yours.** Add photos, a video message and your real signature.
3. **Send in Messages.** It arrives as a bubble they tap to open, right in the chat.

## The Thanksgiving collection

Leaves drift, pumpkins pop in, a turkey gets very excited. Every card plays when it's opened.

## Gifts

A gift stays wrapped until they tap to open it:

- **Gift cards**: send a code with the card
- **Presents**: add a tracking number and it becomes a live link
- **Dinner**: share a food-delivery order so they can follow it
- **AI credits**: paste in a code

## Coming next

## FAQ

**Does the person I send it to need the app?**
No. If they have merci., the card opens inside Messages. If not, or if they're on a Mac, the bubble opens the card in their browser.

**Can I use my own photos and handwriting?**
Yes. Upload photos or a video, then sign with your finger or take a photo of your signature and merci. cleans it up.

**Is my card public?**
No, not unless you choose to share the design. Shared copies have your photos and signature removed.

**Is it in the App Store?**
Not yet. It's coming soon. Until then you can make a card on the web at https://app.merci.cards/.
