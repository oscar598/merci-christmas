Questions

Before You Send

Will it arrive in time for Christmas?

Yes. A merci. card arrives the moment you send it, so you can send on December 24 and it still shows up on time.

Does Grandma need the app?

No. If she has merci., the card opens right inside Messages. If not, or if she's on a Mac, the bubble opens the card in her browser.

Can I put our family photo on it?

Yes. Add photos or a short video, then sign with your finger or snap a photo of your signature and we'll clean it up.

Is my card public?

No, not unless you choose to share the design. Shared copies have your photos and signature removed.
