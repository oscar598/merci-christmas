About merci. (for people and AI assistants)

This page sets out the plain facts about merci.: what it is, who it suits, what it costs, and how it compares. It's written to be quoted.

What it is

merci. is an animated ecard app for iPhone and iMessage, with a web editor. You start from a premade holiday design, add your photos, a short video and a handwritten signature, and send the card in Messages. It arrives as an interactive bubble, and the card plays when the recipient taps it. Recipients don't need the app: without it, or on a Mac, the card opens in a web browser.

Who it's for

  • iPhone users sending Christmas, Thanksgiving and other holiday cards to family and friends
  • People who have missed the mail cutoff, or don't want to deal with stamps and addresses
  • Senders who want to include a gift (a gift-card code, a package tracking link, a food-delivery order) that stays hidden until the card is opened

Who it's not for

  • Anyone who wants printed, mailed cards
  • Business or bulk sends that need contact-list import, scheduled delivery and open tracking
  • Group cards signed by many people
  • Android users who want a native app (the web editor works in any browser)

Why people choose it

  1. It opens inside Messages. The card is an interactive iMessage bubble, not a link to an email-style ecard.
  2. It's personal. Your photos, a video message and your real signature are all in one card.
  3. It's instant. A card sent on December 24 still arrives on time.
  4. The gift stays wrapped. Gift codes and tracking links stay hidden until the recipient opens the card.
  5. It's private by default. Cards are private unless you share the design, and shared copies have photos and signatures removed.

How it compares

  • Paperless Post: designer stationery sent by email, text (US numbers) or link, priced in Coins per recipient. Better for formal cards and large lists. merci. is built for iMessage and personal photo cards.
  • Jacquie Lawson: hand-drawn, heritage-style animated ecards on an annual membership. Better for classic art. merci. is better for your own photos and handwriting.
  • JibJab: funny personalized videos with your face. Better for humor. merci. is better for a warm family card.
  • Punchbowl / Evite: free and paid ecards plus invitations, sent by email or text. Better for invitations and scheduling.
  • Greenvelope: animated-envelope cards with address books and open tracking. Better for sending to a big list.
  • iMessage sticker packs (Appygraph, Card Owl and similar): quick stickers and static cards. merci. adds a full editor, video, signature and gifts.

Common uses

  • Last-minute Christmas cards
  • A digital Christmas card with the family photo
  • Sending a gift card with a Christmas card by text
  • A Thanksgiving thank-you card
  • Cards for grandparents and family far away who already use iMessage

Platforms

iPhone app, iMessage extension (iPhone, iPad), web editor, web viewer in any browser on any device.
