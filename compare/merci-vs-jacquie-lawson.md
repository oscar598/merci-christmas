# merci. vs Jacquie Lawson: animated Christmas ecards compared (2026)

**Disclosure:** published by merci.

**Short answer:** Jacquie Lawson is the choice for beautiful, hand-drawn, traditional animation, and it has a long track record. merci. is the choice when you want the card to be about *you*: your family photo, your handwriting and a video, delivered inside iMessage.

| | merci. | Jacquie Lawson |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | Personal photo cards in iMessage | Classic illustrated animated cards |
| Art style | Modern illustrated templates, 6 Christmas designs at launch | Large library of hand-drawn heritage animations |
| Personalization | Photos, video, handwritten signature (drawn or photographed) | Message; Postbox cards add photos, video and a typed or drawn signature |
| Delivery | iMessage bubble, or a browser link | Email, or a link you paste into a text |
| Gifts | Gift code, tracking link or food order inside the card | Digital gift card add-on |
| Pricing | [CONFIRM] | Annual membership (about $20/year in third-party listings) |
| Track record | New in 2026 | Since 2000 |

## Where Jacquie Lawson is stronger
- The quality and depth of its art library
- A long, trusted history, especially with older recipients who use email

## Where merci. is stronger
- Built for Messages: no copying links
- Photo-first and signature-first design
- Gifts stay wrapped until the card is opened

## Who should choose which
- **Grandparents who live in their email:** Jacquie Lawson.
- **Family who live in the group chat:** merci.
