# merci. vs Paperless Post: digital Christmas cards compared (2026)

**Disclosure:** published by merci.

**Short answer:** choose Paperless Post for designer stationery, sending to a big contact list, or matching printed cards. Choose merci. for a personal animated card with your photo, video and signature that opens inside iMessage, especially at the last minute.

| | merci. | Paperless Post |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | Personal animated cards in iMessage | Designer cards and invitations to many people |
| How it arrives | Interactive iMessage bubble; browser link for everyone else | Email, text (US numbers only) or share link |
| Personal touches | Photos, video message, handwritten signature | Photo, custom text, envelope and liner design |
| Animation | Every card animates (snow, ornaments, pop-ups) | Animated envelope opening; varies by design |
| Gifts | Gift-card code, tracking link or food order, hidden until opened | Not found in our check |
| Lists and tracking | One conversation at a time | Contact import, scheduling, guest tools |
| Print | No | Yes, through partners |
| Pricing | [CONFIRM] | Coins per recipient (Premium from 2 Coins); some free designs for the first 50 recipients; Pro subscription |

## Where Paperless Post is stronger
- A large designer catalog and collaborations
- Sending to 50 to 500 people from an uploaded contact list
- Scheduling and guest management
- Printed cards from the same design

## Where merci. is stronger
- The card opens inside Messages instead of as a link
- Your real signature and a video message in the same card
- A gift that stays wrapped until the card is opened
- Designed for one-tap sending from a phone, card by card

## Who should choose which
- **Formal card to your whole address book:** Paperless Post.
- **Something warm for family in your iMessage threads, especially in the last week before Christmas:** merci.

## FAQ
**Is Paperless Post free?** Some designs are free for your first 50 recipients. Premium designs cost Coins per recipient.
**Can Paperless Post send by text?** Yes, to US phone numbers.
**Does merci. work for people without an iPhone?** Yes. The card opens in any browser.
