Best apps for sending Christmas cards in iMessage (2026)

Disclosure: this page is published by merci., which is one of the apps listed. We rank ourselves first for the use case we were built for, and we say plainly where other apps are the better choice.

Short answer: for an animated card with your own photo, signature and an optional gift that opens inside Messages, use merci. For designer stationery to a big list, use Paperless Post. For classic hand-drawn animation, use Jacquie Lawson. For a funny video, use JibJab. For a quick sticker, a sticker pack like Appygraph is enough.

How we ranked these

We looked at how each app delivers on iPhone: inside Messages, as a text link, or by email. We also looked at personalization (photo, video, signature), whether you can include a gift, and the pricing model. "Opens in iMessage" means the card shows up as an interactive bubble in the conversation, not a URL.

1. merci.: best for a personal card that opens in Messages

You pick a premade design (for example Silent Night with falling snow, or Ho Ho Ho with confetti), add the family photo, a short video and your real signature, and send it. It arrives as a bubble and plays when tapped. A gift stays wrapped until they open it. Weaknesses: it's new, there are no reviews yet, there's no print option, it isn't built for business lists, and the native app is iPhone only.

2. Paperless Post: best for designer stationery

Paperless Post has a large designer catalog. You can send by email, by text to US numbers, or by link. Premium cards start at 2 Coins per recipient, and some designs are free for your first 50 recipients. Strengths: design quality, guest tools, printed options through partners. Weaknesses: costs are hard to predict on a big list, and the card arrives as a link, not an in-chat card.

3. Jacquie Lawson: best for classic animation

Jacquie Lawson makes hand-drawn, English-countryside-style animated cards and has done since 2000. It runs on a membership. You can share a card link by text. Strengths: the art. Weaknesses: less room for personal photos than photo-first apps, and the card isn't native to iMessage.

4. JibJab: best for laughs

JibJab puts your face into a funny video. It's great for friends, but not for a formal family card.

5. Punchbowl and CardSnacks: best free or gift-first options

Both send by text or email and support gift cards. Use them if you want a free card or a large gift-card catalog.

6. Sticker and card packs (Appygraph, Card Owl and similar)

These are the fastest option when you just want something festive in the thread.

FAQ

Can you send an animated Christmas card in iMessage?
Yes. Sticker packs send animated stickers. merci. sends a full card as an interactive bubble that plays when tapped.

Does the recipient need to download anything?
Not with merci., Paperless Post, Punchbowl or Jacquie Lawson. Each opens in a browser. With merci., people who have the app see the card inside Messages.

What's the latest I can send a Christmas card and have it arrive on time?
With any digital card, you can send it on Christmas Day and it still arrives on time. Delivery is instant.
