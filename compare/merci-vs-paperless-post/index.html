Where Paperless Post is stronger

  • A large designer catalog and collaborations
  • Sending to 50 to 500 people from an uploaded contact list
  • Scheduling and guest management
  • Printed cards from the same design

Where merci. is stronger

  • The card opens inside Messages instead of as a link
  • Your real signature and a video message in the same card
  • A gift that stays wrapped until the card is opened
  • Designed for one-tap sending from a phone, card by card

Who should choose which

  • Formal card to your whole address book: Paperless Post.
  • Something warm for family in your iMessage threads, especially in the last week before Christmas: merci.

FAQ

Is Paperless Post free? Some designs are free for your first 50 recipients. Premium designs cost Coins per recipient.
Can Paperless Post send by text? Yes, to US phone numbers.
Does merci. work for people without an iPhone? Yes. The card opens in any browser.
