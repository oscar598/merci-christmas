# How to send a Christmas card with a gift card inside, by text (2026)

**Disclosure:** published by merci.

**Short answer:** you have three routes. (1) Buy an eGift card with text delivery from the retailer, then send a card separately. (2) Use an ecard service with gift cards, such as CardSnacks, Punchbowl, Jacquie Lawson or American Greetings, and send its link by text. (3) Use merci., where the gift goes inside an animated card that opens in iMessage and stays wrapped until it's tapped.

| Option | Card and gift together? | Arrives as | Gift types |
|---|---|---|---|
| Retailer eGift by text (for example Amazon) | No, the card is separate | A text with a code or link | That retailer only |
| CardSnacks | Yes | A text link to the card | 400+ retailers |
| Punchbowl | Yes | Text or email | Gift cards |
| Jacquie Lawson | Yes (email send) | Email | Digital gift cards from many retailers |
| merci. | Yes | An iMessage bubble; browser for others | Gift code, present tracking, food-delivery order, AI credits |

## Step by step with merci.
1. Pick a Christmas design and add your photo and signature.
2. Open the **Gift** tab and add a gift card, a tracking number for a present you shipped, or a food-delivery order.
3. Send it in Messages. The gift shows as "wrapped" until they tap to open the card.

## Tips
- Treat a gift-card code like cash. Send it only to the right person.
- For family far away, a shared food-delivery order on Christmas Day goes over well.
