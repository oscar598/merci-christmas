# merci. Christmas: animated Christmas cards that open in iMessage

merci. is an iPhone and web app for making animated Christmas cards and sending them by iMessage. You design the card on your phone, sign it by hand, add photos, a video or a gift, and it arrives instantly as a bubble the recipient taps to open.

## Why send a merci. card instead of paper

| | Paper Christmas cards | merci. |
|---|---|---|
| What you need | Cards, stamps, envelopes, addresses | Their phone number, already in Messages |
| Deadline | Mail by mid-December to be safe | Send on December 24 and it still arrives on time |
| Delivery | Sometimes cards get lost | Arrives the moment you send it |
| Photos | Only if you print them | Photos, a video message and your real signature |
| Time per card | A weekend for the whole list | About 2 minutes from a premade design |

## How it works

1. **Pick a Christmas design.** Quiet and classic, or snow, confetti and candy canes.
2. **Make it yours.** Add the family photo, a short video message and your signature, drawn with a finger or photographed and cleaned up.
3. **Send it in Messages.** It arrives as a bubble they tap to open. Then you move on to the next person on the list.

## The Christmas collection

Every card plays when it's opened: ornaments swing in, snow falls, a tree pops up with gifts.

- **Merry & Bright**: ornaments swing in on deep red

- **Ho Ho Ho**: confetti, candy canes, full volume

## A gift inside the card

A gift stays wrapped until the recipient taps to open the card. Codes, tracking numbers and links stay hidden until then.

- **Gift cards**: send a code with the card
- **Presents**: add a tracking number and they can follow the package
- **Christmas dinner**: share a food-delivery order with family far away
- **AI credits**: for the person who has everything

## FAQ

**Will it arrive in time for Christmas?**
Yes. A merci. card arrives the moment you send it, so a card sent on December 24 is still on time.

**Does the recipient need the app?**
No. If they have merci., the card opens inside Messages. If not, or if they're on a Mac, the bubble opens the card in their browser.

**Can I put our family photo on it?**
Yes. Add photos or a short video, then sign with your finger or take a photo of your signature and merci. cleans it up.

**Is my card public?**
No, not unless you choose to share the design. Shared copies have your photos and signature removed.
